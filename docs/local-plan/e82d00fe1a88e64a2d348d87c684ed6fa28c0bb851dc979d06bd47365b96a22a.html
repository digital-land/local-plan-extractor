Notes
This is a National Park with special statutory purposes focusing on conservation. The plan follows a needs-led rural exceptions approach prioritizing affordable housing for local communities only. No specific housing targets or allocations are set - housing is only permitted where local need is demonstrated. The approach is explicitly not to provide general housing targets as National Parks are not suitable locations for unrestricted housing development.
