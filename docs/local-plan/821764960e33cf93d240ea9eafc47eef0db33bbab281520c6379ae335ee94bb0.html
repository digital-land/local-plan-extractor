Notes
Total requirement 16,500 homes over plan period. Windfall allowance of 2,000 dwellings (50 per year rural areas + 50 per year urban areas). Strategic allocations include Hereford (6,500), market towns (4,700), and rural settlements (5,300). Stepped targets: 600 dpa (2011-2016), 850 dpa (2016-2021), 900 dpa (2021-2026), 950 dpa (2026-2031).
